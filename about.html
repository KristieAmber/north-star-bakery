Our Story

North Star Bakery started with a love for baking and bringing people together. We wanted to create a place where neighbors could stop in for fresh bread, a morning pastry, or something sweet for a special occasion.

Our goal is to make everyone feel welcome and provide fresh baked goods that people can enjoy with their families.

Our Ingredients

We believe good baking starts with good ingredients. We try to use fresh, quality ingredients and support local suppliers whenever possible. We also believe in keeping our recipes simple and making our products with care.

Meet Our Team

Our team is made up of bakers and friendly staff who enjoy what they do. Our bakers start early each morning preparing bread and pastries, while our front counter staff help customers find their favorites.
